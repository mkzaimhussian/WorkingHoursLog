# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Everyone is welcome to use, discuss and contribute to Working Hours Log, whatever their background, identity, experience level or nationality. We want this project to be friendly, respectful and free from harassment.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to coding or to GitHub.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences; disagree about ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Respect privacy: never post other people's personal information, such as timesheet data, client names, emails or phone numbers.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, personal attacks, trolling or deliberately derogatory comments.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions and commit messages. It also applies when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please contact the maintainer, [@mkzaimhussian](https://github.com/mkzaimhussian), through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. Please do not report conduct issues in a public issue.

## Enforcement

The maintainer will review every report and may respond in whatever way is appropriate and proportionate, for example:

1. **Correction:** a private message explaining why the behaviour was inappropriate.
2. **Warning:** a warning, with consequences for continued behaviour.
3. **Temporary ban:** a temporary restriction from interacting with the project.
4. **Permanent ban:** a permanent ban for serious or repeated violations.

Comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code of conduct may be edited or removed.
